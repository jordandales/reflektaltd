The place

The village sits near Greatham Creek, which runs towards the Tees at Seal Sands. Two buildings mark it: the Hospital of God, the old almshouses founded in the 13th century and still a set of homes, and the church of St John the Baptist. Around them are the older village houses and, further out, later streets of family homes.

Reflekta Ltd’s registered office is 14 Queensway, Greatham, TS25 2HL. That is a working address, not a shop you call into. Book by the form or by phone, the same as everywhere else we cover.

Cleaning here

Visits are monthly or fortnightly. Outside we use a pure water-fed pole and traditional methods where they fit. Glass, frames and sills are included, and you do not need to be in.
