Hartlepool window cleaning

Window cleaning in Hart Village

Hart is a small village north-west of Hartlepool, separate from the town centre, and we clean windows there on a monthly or fortnightly round.

Window cleaning from £15

Join the round Call 07377 987124

The place

St Mary Magdalene is the old church at the centre of the village, on a front street of older houses. Newer homes sit around that core, still on a village scale, with open country beyond. It is a different clean from a Seaton Carew seafront house or a Fens semi: cottage windows, painted timber and stone sit next to later houses.

We use a pure water-fed pole or a traditional clean depending on the window, and we agree that before we start. Frames, sills and glass are included. You do not need to be home, and we send photos afterwards.

One-off work

Gutters, driveways and solar panels are quoted separately when you need them. The only published price is window cleaning from £15.
