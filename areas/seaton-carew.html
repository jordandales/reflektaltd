The place

The beach runs the length of the resort, with the promenade heading north towards Hartlepool Marina. Holy Trinity Church stands a short walk from the sand. South of the front, the dunes sit beside Seaton Carew Golf Club, the old links course.

Houses here are a mix. There are older streets facing the sea, and family houses and bungalows set back from the prom. Salt and spray mark glass faster than they do a mile inland, so a fortnightly visit is often the better gap beside the sea. Tell us which you want when you book.

What we do on a visit

Outside, we use a pure water-fed pole, and traditional methods where they suit the window. Frames, sills and glass are included. Inside is available too. You do not need to be in. We send photos when we have finished.

If the driveway, the gutters or the solar panels need doing, those are quoted as their own jobs. They are not hidden in the window price. Window cleaning starts from £15. We do not publish other prices.
