Monthly or fortnightly: choosing a window cleaning round in Hartlepool

6 November 2026 · Reflekta Ltd

Reflekta’s window cleaning rounds are monthly or fortnightly. Both are recurring. You are not booking a fresh one-off every time, and you do not need to be home for the outside. That is the whole of the choice we offer. This note is to help a Hartlepool household decide which gap to ask for. It is not a case study, it does not quote a customer, and it does not pretend we have counted how dirty the average street gets.

Window cleaning from £15 is the only price published on this site. We are not adding a second figure for “fortnightly” or “monthly” here. The quote for your house depends on the property. You get that by email, usually the same day, with no obligation. If the two rhythms are not the same price for your place, the quote will say so. Do not infer a discount, or a surcharge, from this page.

What stays the same either way

Whichever gap you pick, a visit on our window cleaning round covers the glass, the frames and the sills. Outside, the usual method is a water-fed pole. Pure water has the minerals filtered out, so it rinses the glass and dries without spots. Where a traditional clean suits the window better, that is what we use, including inside if you have asked for it. We send photos when the job is finished, which is the point of not having to be in.

The round itself is the other constant. You tell us the rhythm once. We come back on that schedule, so you are not starting from scratch with a new booking each month. Heavy rain and strong wind can still move a visit. They make a streak-free finish difficult, and they make work at height less safe. If we cannot do the job properly on the planned day, we contact you to rearrange. That applies to both cadences.

Payment does not change either. We email a Square invoice when the work is done. You pay by card through the link. It is secured by Square. We do not take cash on the doorstep. We are fully insured, and we arrive in uniform. None of that is a reason to pick one gap over the other. It is simply how the round works.

When a month is often enough

A monthly visit is four weeks between cleans, more or less, depending on how the calendar falls and on weather. For many houses set back from the sea, that is plenty. On the Fens, in Greatham and in Hart Village, salt spray is less of a daily fact than it is on the Seaton Carew front. Dust, rain spots and the ordinary film of a residential street are what you are mostly looking at. If the glass still looks acceptable in the week before we are due, monthly is a sensible ask.

Look at the house, not at a rule. If you can see a haze when the sun hits the front, if the sills feel gritty, or if you are wiping a spyhole in the glass to see out, the gap may be longer than you like. That can happen on a monthly round without meaning the round is “wrong”. It means you might prefer the shorter one. It can also happen because the house faces a busy road, or because trees drop onto the sills, which has nothing to do with the coast.

We will not tell you that an inland Hartlepool house “must” be monthly. A household that simply likes the glass cleaner, or that has a lot of glazing, can ask for fortnightly anywhere we cover. The address tells us where you are. It does not choose the rhythm for you.

When fortnightly is often the better ask

Fortnightly is the shorter of the two rounds. It is the one people by the sea often prefer, because salt and spray can show again before four weeks are up. The Seaton Carew note goes into sea air, weather and seagull marks. The short version is: a house facing the beach usually works the glass harder than a house on the Fens. The shorter gap often suits that. It is not a requirement, and we have no statistic for how many seafront homes do which.

Fortnightly is also worth considering if you already know you notice the windows halfway through a month, wherever you live. Conservatories, low sun on a south-facing front, and pale frames that show every mark are ordinary reasons. None of them is a diagnosis we can make from a blog. Mention them when you enquire and we can quote the rhythm you actually want.

What fortnightly is not: a promise that the glass will look newly cleaned on day thirteen. Birds, building dust and a wet week can all land between visits. A round reduces how long that sits there. It does not freeze the house in the state we left it.

How to decide without overthinking it

Start with what you see now. If the windows are only mildly dull and you are not on the seafront, ask for monthly. If you are on the Seaton Carew side of town, or you already wipe the glass yourself more than once a month, ask for fortnightly. If you are unsure, say so. We would rather quote the option you name than invent a schedule for a house we have not quoted.
