Getting Hartlepool gutters ready for winter

6 November 2026 · Reflekta Ltd

Hartlepool’s wetter months are when a neglected gutter stops being a background detail. Leaves, moss and grit that sat quietly in summer get a proper test the first time rain comes in off the North Sea and the gutter cannot carry it. This note is about spotting that, about what a clearance can help with, and about not climbing a ladder you are not set up to use. It is not a promise that a clean gutter prevents every damp patch, or every bit of damage a house can suffer.

Gutter maintenance with Reflekta is a one-off. It is not part of the monthly or fortnightly window cleaning round. Window cleaning starts from £15. A gutter clean is quoted for the house, once we know the address.

What tends to block them here

The usual contents are dull: leaves, moss, seed and the fine grit that wind moves along a roof. On the Seaton Carew side, salt and wind add to that mix, which we already see as a reason the outside of those houses works harder. On the Fens, and around Greatham and Hart Village, it is more often the ordinary debris of trees, moss from the tiles, and a season of rain. None of that needs a dramatic explanation. A gutter is a channel. If the channel is full, the water goes somewhere else.

Autumn is the obvious time to look, before the longer wet spells. Not every house needs a clearance every October. A house under heavy trees may want a look twice in a leafy year. A house with little overhead growth may go longer. If you are unsure, describe the roof when you ask and we will quote from that, then confirm on the day.

Signs worth taking seriously

You can often see trouble from the ground, which is where you should stay. Water spilling over the front edge of the gutter in a steady rain is the plainest sign. A downpipe that drips from a joint, or that barely runs at the bottom while the gutter above is overflowing, suggests the pipe or the outlet is holding debris. Plants growing out of the gutter are not a charm. They mean the trough has been damp and dirty long enough to root something.

Staining on the wall under a joint, or a dark streak below a fascia, can mean water has been running down the face of the house instead of into the downpipe. Inside, a damp patch high on an external wall after heavy rain can have the same cause. It can also have other causes: failed pointing, a cracked tile, condensation, or a leak that has nothing to do with the gutter. Clearing the gutter removes one common route for overflow. It does not diagnose the wall, and it does not put right damage that is already done.

If the gutter itself is split, pulled off its brackets, or sloping the wrong way, a clearance will not fix the fall. We will tell you what we find. We clear, we check the downpipes, and we can clean the fascia, which is often as marked as the glass. We do not dress that up as a building survey.

Why a clearance helps, and what it does not promise

A blocked gutter can overflow. Water running down the wall is a common way for paint and masonry to stay wet, and wet masonry is one of the ordinary routes towards damp. Letting rain leave the roof the way the gutter was fitted to do reduces that risk. A clear gutter does not prevent every kind of damp, or every bit of winter damage. Houses get damp for reasons a vacuum on the gutter cannot touch.

The same modesty applies to leaks indoors. If water is already coming in, say so when you book. We can still clear the run and tell you what we see. We will not promise the ceiling will dry because the trough is empty.

Leave the ladder

Clearing gutters means working at height, often over a drive, a path or a narrow side passage. Ladders on uneven or wet ground, used alone, are how people get hurt. A domestic ladder leant against a plastic gutter is also a good way to damage the gutter you were trying to help. If you are not set up for that work, with a stable footing and someone who knows what they are doing, do not turn a Saturday into an accident.

We would rather you asked us. Gutter maintenance is the job: vacuum clearance, a check of the downpipes, fascia cleaning when it is part of the visit, and a straightforward account of what we found. You do not need to be home. We send photos when it is finished. Tell us about access, especially a tight passage or a conservatory underneath, when you enquire. We quote from the address and what you describe, then confirm on the day.

The same booking steps apply as for windows. Get in touch, get a quote by email usually the same day, we do the work, and a Square invoice arrives for you to pay by card. No cash on the doorstep. We are fully insured. Heavy weather can move the day, for the same reason it moves a window visit: a decent finish and safe working come first.

Where this sits next to the window round

If you are already on a monthly or fortnightly window round in Seaton Carew, the Fens, Hart Village or Greatham, the gutters are still a separate request. We do not add them silently to the window price. Mention them on the form, or call 07377 987124. The questions page covers the round itself if the windows are the part you are still deciding.

Use the quote form and choose gutter maintenance if that is the job. Say which elevation, whether you have already seen overflow, and whether anyone has been up there recently. A clear description makes a clearer quote. The aim before winter is simple: rain should leave the roof through the gutter, not down the wall, and nobody in the house should be the person on the ladder.
