Solar panel cleaning in Hartlepool

Dirt, bird droppings and debris sit on the glass and get in the way of the light. We clean solar panels with a pure water system, and we treat the roof as a place to work carefully.

Get a solar panel quote Call 07377 987124

What we do

The clean uses pure water, the same idea as the window round: water with the minerals taken out, so the glass rinses clear and dries without spots. We are not publishing a percentage gain. Clean glass simply lets the panels do the job they were fitted for.

Safety comes first. If the roof, the weather or the access is not right for a proper clean, we will say so rather than rush it. You do not need to be home. We send photos when the work is finished.

  • Pure water cleaning
  • Dirt, bird droppings and debris removed
  • Care taken on the roof
  • Photos on completion

Booking it

Solar panel cleaning is a one-off, like pressure washing and gutters. It is not part of the window cleaning round, and it does not have a published price. Window cleaning is the only figure on the site: from £15. Ask for a quote and we will price the array you actually have.

Hartlepool roofs, including Seaton Carew where gulls are part of life by the beach, are the work we know. The Fens, Hart Village and Greatham are covered too.
