Gutter cleaning in Hartlepool

Blocked gutters cause damp, leaks and structural damage. We clear them, clean them and inspect them. Gutter maintenance is booked on its own, not as part of the monthly window round.

Get a gutter quote Call 07377 987124

What the visit covers

Leaves, moss and grit settle in the gutter and then in the downpipe. A clearance takes that out so rain can leave the roof the way it should. While we are there we check the downpipes and we can clean the fascia, which is the board behind the gutter and is often as marked as the glass.

  • Vacuum clearance of the gutters
  • Fascia cleaning
  • Downpipe checks
  • A look over the run so we can tell you what we found
