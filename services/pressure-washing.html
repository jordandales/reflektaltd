Pressure washing in Hartlepool

Driveways, patios, paths and walls pick up dirt, moss, algae and grime. Pressure washing is how we clear them. It is a bigger, one-off job, not part of the window cleaning round.

What we wash

The work covers driveways and patios, paths, brickwork and conservatory valets. Years of dirt sit in the texture of concrete and paving, and a rinse with a hose does not shift it. A pressure washer does.

In Seaton Carew the sea air and damp encourage green growth on paths and patio edges. On estates such as the Fens, the usual job is a tired driveway or a patio that has gone dark. We look at the surface before we quote, because a quote depends on the size and the condition.

  • Driveways and patios
  • Paths and walls
  • Brickwork
  • Conservatory valets

How it differs from window cleaning

Window cleaning from £15 is the regular round. Pressure washing is priced on the job, after we know what we are washing. We do not publish a price list for it. You get a quote first, with no obligation.
