What a visit includes

We clean inside and out for homes and businesses. The usual outside clean is done with a pure water-fed pole, which reaches upper windows from the ground. Where a traditional clean suits the window better, that is what we use.

Glass, frames and sills are all part of the clean, so you are not left with bright glass and a dirty frame. The aim is a streak-free finish. When the job is done we send photos, which is useful if you were out.

  • External and internal window cleaning
  • Pure water-fed poles and traditional methods
  • Frames, sills and glass
  • Photos when the work is finished

The round

Window cleaning is the main work. Most visits are a set round, monthly or fortnightly, across Hartlepool and Seaton Carew. You pick the gap when you book. After that we come back on schedule, so you are not starting again each time.

Pressure washing, gutter maintenance and solar panel cleaning are separate, bigger jobs. Ask for them if you want them, but they are not bundled into the window price.
